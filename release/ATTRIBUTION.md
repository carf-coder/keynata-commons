## Takedown policy

Every track is generated by our own rule-and-statistics engine and screened
against its training corpus for distinctive matching passages before
release. If you believe a track nevertheless reproduces protected material,
open an issue on the GitHub repository with the track ID and the work you
believe is affected. We will investigate and remove the track while the
question is open. Removed tracks stay removed.
